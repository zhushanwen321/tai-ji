Adds the plan-review marker protocol (`PLAN_REVIEW_MARKER`) and the scheduler-create interactive protocol (`SCHEDULE_CREATE_MARKER`), plus a new `scheduler-create` submodule with schedule draft helpers (`dateToOnceCron`, `onceCronToDate`, `isScheduleDraft`) and `PlanReview` shared types for the plan review workflow.
